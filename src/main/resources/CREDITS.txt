EbreduxAddon - credits

Code
- Built on Electroblob's Wizardry Redux (Binaris00, GPL-3.0-only),
  https://github.com/Binaris00/ElectroblobsWizardryRedux
- Spell and item design ported from Insane Tweaks for Minecraft 1.12.2 (spege / Isuth).

Assets
- data/ebreduxaddon/structures/empty_3x3x3.nbt: copied from Electroblob's Wizardry Redux (GPL-3.0-only).
- Item, armour and effect textures: carried over from Insane Tweaks 1.12.2. Its credits
  apply unchanged: "Core" item textures are modified assets from the Faithful 32x Resource Pack
  (Faithful Team, distributed under their community license); the battlemage armour textures are
  based on Electroblob's Wizardry battlemage textures (Electroblob's Wizardry Team).
- Armour model layers (textures/models/armor/*_layer_1/2.png) are the top 64x32 of the 1.12.2
  64x64 battlemage model textures - placeholders until proper vanilla-layout layers exist.
- Spell icons: Redux spell icons (GPL-3.0-only) with a hue shift inside the frame, generated by
  tools/spell_icons.py (cleanse <- cure_effects, purifying_pulse <- healing_aura,
  grasp <- entrapment, spine_volley <- dart). The 1.12.2 icons were placeholders themselves.
- The spine projectile renders with Redux's dart texture.
